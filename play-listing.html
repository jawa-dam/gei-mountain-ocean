About this game

TAP WATER. MAKE WAVES. SAVE THE ‑ITES.

Explore the mountain, operate the dam, power the waterwheel, earn FL OZ rewards, and journey through an imaginative six-part STEM learning adventure from mountain to ocean.

TapLites turns real hydraulic ideas into a fast, tactile tap game. Each level follows water through six stations — every tap moves the flow, every completed day banks FL OZ, and every level ends at the factory your water helped power.

Along the way you rescue the trapped -ites, unlock characters and songs, and discover how reservoirs store energy, how gates control pressure and how a waterwheel turns flow into work — part of the Genesis Engineered Interpretations (GEI) Divide · Assign · Manage framework.

  • DAY 1Mountain
  • DAY 2Dam
  • DAY 3Millpond
  • DAY 4Sluice Gate
  • DAY 5Waterwheel
  • DAY 6Factory
  • 💧Interactive hydraulics. Tap the glowing station to move water through the system and keep your flow combo alive.
  • 🗺️DAM MAP. Follow the water from the mountain peak and track the factories your levels bring online.
  • 🏆Progression & rewards. Earn 111 FL OZ per day and 666 per level, with milestone celebrations as you advance.
  • 🎡BONUS FLOW Waterwheel. Milestone levels unlock a free spin for FL OZ, songs and crew.
  • 🎰DAM MACHINE. A free hydraulic arcade — three reels, zero dam breaks — played with earned free plays.
  • 📖Educational storytelling. Narrated voices, a Beaver buddy guide and STEM notes tie each day to a real engineering idea.
