Build a hydroelectric dam from mountain to ocean in this tap-powered STEM adventure!

TapLites: GEI Mountain Ocean follows the Genesis Engineered Interpretations story through six repeating Days of creation and engineering. Tap to shape mountains, construct dams, fill millponds, open sluice gates, spin waterwheels, and power factories — all driven by a shared hydraulic simulation that models real water flow.

SIX DAYS OF ENGINEERING
• Day 1 — Mountain: Shape the terrain where your dam will stand
• Day 2 — Dam: Construct the dam wall block by block
• Day 3 — Millpond: Fill the reservoir behind your dam
• Day 4 — Sluice Gate: Control water flow with precision gates
• Day 5 — Waterwheel: Spin the wheel with flowing water (BONUS FLOW!)
• Day 6 — Factory: Power the factory and light up the DAM MACHINE

REAL HYDRAULIC SIMULATION
Every tap interacts with a shared water simulation. Water flows downhill, fills basins, drives turbines, and generates power — all calculated in real time.

3D PREMIUM SCENES
Experience each Day in detailed 3D with per-scene atmosphere, materials, and lighting. Built on Three.js with automatic SVG fallback for older devices.

DAM MAP
Navigate between all six Days from the interactive DAM MAP. Track your progress and revisit completed stages.

TAPLITES ARCADE
Unlock mini-games as you progress through the Days.

PROGRESSION & REWARDS
• XP — Earn experience with every tap
• FL OZ — Collect fluid-ounce currency for premium content
• Builder Parts — Gather construction materials for upgrades

EDUCATIONAL STORYTELLING
Learn about hydroelectric power, water management, and civil engineering through interactive gameplay rooted in the Genesis creation narrative.

IN-APP PURCHASES
Optional FL OZ packs and special character unlocks. No ads. No subscriptions. No pay-to-win mechanics — every Day is completable without purchases.

FAITH & LEARNING
TapLites weaves engineering concepts with the Genesis story, offering a unique perspective where STEM education meets faith-based storytelling.

Built with care by YallToo.
