Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

TapLites: GEI Mountain Ocean ("the Game") is published by YallToo / Genesis Engineered Interpretations ("we", "us"). This policy describes what data the Game collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Data We Collect

The Game is designed to minimize data collection.

  • Game progress. Levels completed, FL OZ balance, unlocked items, and Builder Parts are stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you make a purchase.
  • Purchase data. When you make an in-app purchase, your transaction is processed by PayPal (or, on Android, Google Play Billing). We receive a transaction ID, the item purchased, and the amount paid. We do not receive or store your payment card number, bank account, or PayPal password.
  • Audio assets. Voice clips and music are streamed from a content delivery network. Standard HTTP request logs (IP address, timestamp, requested file) may be recorded by the CDN provider.
  • Hosting logs. The Game is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may collect standard server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and pages visited, subject to Vercel's Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number during gameplay.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or advertising networks.
  • We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.
  • The Game contains no ads.

3. Children's Privacy

The Game is an educational STEM game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because the Game does not require accounts or collect identifying information during gameplay, children can play without providing personal data. Purchases require a payment method controlled by an adult.

4. Third-Party Services

  • PayPal processes web purchases. See PayPal's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing processes Android in-app purchases. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel hosts the web application. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

Game progress is stored on your device and persists until you clear your browser data or uninstall the app. Purchase entitlement records are retained on our server for up to 365 days to support purchase recovery and customer support.

6. Your Rights

You can delete your local game data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain or by uninstalling the Android app. For questions about purchase records, contact us at contact@yalltoo.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
