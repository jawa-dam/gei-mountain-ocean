Learn the flow, choose your avatar, then start the hydraulic system.

👆 Tap the glowing station. The active station glows. Tap anywhere on the board to work it — your first tap starts the day's clock.
📊 Watch the HUD. It shows your level, day, FL OZ, the countdown timer, and the -ite you're rescuing.
🏆 Complete all six days. Finish each station before the timer runs out to move the water from the Mountain to the Ocean.
💧 Earn FL OZ. Every finished day adds 111 FL OZ to your wallet — 666 FL OZ for a full level.

👤 Characters. Two starters are free. Buy the rest with FL OZ in 👤 Characters, or win them in the bonus games.
📈 Progression. Each level asks for more taps, saves another -ite, and grows your mill. Big celebrations wait at milestone levels.
